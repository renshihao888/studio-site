Mat cutting calculator for picture framers — openings, borders, reveal and rabbet stack math.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation runs on-device. The only network use is the App Store purchase flow for the optional Pro unlock.

How do I enter fractions?

Type them as you say them: 7-3/4, 1/2 or decimals like 7.75. Results display in fractional inches.

What overlap and reveal should I use?

Defaults are 1/8–1/4" overlap per side; every value is editable. Conventions vary by shop and moulding — the app shows the math so you can check it against your own standards before cutting.

Where are my saved jobs?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot see or recover them.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the About tab → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID; there is no subscription.
