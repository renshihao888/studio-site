Frequently asked questions

How does TrueEase pick my size?

It uses the Craft Yarn Council standard body measurement charts (women XS–5X, men S–5X). Those are actual body measurements, not finished garment sizes. Your bust/chest measurement is matched to the published range. If you fall in a gap between sizes, TrueEase recommends the larger size so you can shape it down.

Why is the finished bust larger than my body?

Because of ease. The CYC Fit & Ease chart defines: very close = 2–4 in less than bust; close = your bust; classic = 2–4 in more; loose = 4–6 in more; oversized = 6 in or more. The app adds the selected ease to your body measurement. Free includes classic fit; Pro unlocks all five.

Why doesn't my cast-on match the pattern?

Patterns are written for the designer's gauge. TrueEase recalculates from your washed-and-blocked 4 in / 10 cm swatch, then rounds up to your pattern multiple. Two knitters with different gauges should cast on different stitch counts for the same finished bust.

The raglan warning says the increases need more rows than the armhole depth. What now?

At your gauge, the CYC armhole depth for that size is shallower than the yoke needs to reach the body stitch count. Options: deepen the armhole, raise the neck cast-on, or work more than 8 increases per round. The app shows both constraints instead of silently picking one.

How is the yarn estimate calculated?

From your own swatch: unravel a measured swatch, note the yards it used and the stitches it contained. TrueEase multiplies your project's total stitches by that yards-per-stitch ratio and adds the buffer you choose (10% by default). It also shows the published CYC yarn-weight sweater range as a sanity check. Cables and stranded colourwork use more yarn — raise the buffer.

What does Pro unlock?

All five CYC fit presets, four constructions (pullover, cardigan, raglan, circular yoke), every increase/decrease step spaced for your gauge, yarn recalculation and PDF export. Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the About tab → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pro is tied to your Apple ID; there is no subscription.

Where do the numbers come from?

Craft Yarn Council Standard Body Measurements/Sizing and the Bust/Chest Fit & Ease chart; the Knitting & Crochet Guild line-sum (“Magic Formula”) for increase/decrease spacing; TKGA's top-down raglan lesson for the 8-stitches-every-other-row construction. Every formula is shown in the app and in About → Sources.

Does it guarantee a fit?

No. It is a planning estimate. Gauge changes after washing, yarn behaves differently, and personal fit is personal. Swatch, measure a sweater you already like, and treat the plan as a starting point.
