Privacy Policy — TrueEase

Effective date: November 28, 2026. Developer: TrueTakeoff Studio (independent).

What we collect

Nothing. TrueEase:

  • works fully offline;
  • has no user accounts and no sign-in;
  • contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs;
  • does not access contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, or health data;
  • does not store or transmit your body measurements, gauge, swatch or plan — they stay on screen unless you export a PDF yourself.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase entitlement is verified on-device through Apple's StoreKit. TrueEase Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

PDF export

If you export a plan, the PDF is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is entirely your choice; we receive nothing.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
