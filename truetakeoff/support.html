Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation runs on-device. The only network use is the App Store purchase flow for the optional Pro unlock.

How do I enter fractions?

Type them as you say them: 3-1/2, 1/2, 2' 6" or decimals like 3.5. Results display in 1/16" fractions (metric toggle available).

Why do takeoffs differ from my fittings?

Fitting dimensions vary by brand (±1/8" is common). TrueTakeoff shows the formula and source for every number (ASME B16.3 center-to-end, B1.20.1 thread engagement). Always verify against your actual fittings before cutting.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the About tab → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID; there is no subscription.
