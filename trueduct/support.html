Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation runs on-device. The only network use is the App Store purchase flow for the optional Pro unlock.

What is the sizing based on?

Round metal duct sizing uses the ASHRAE friction chart basis, with altitude & temperature correction via US Standard Atmosphere density ratios. Pro adds flex duct & duct board sizing (ASHRAE Table 1-2 roughness) with measured compression correction.

Why does my ductulator disagree slightly?

Printed ductulators read a chart by eye; TrueDuct solves the underlying relation numerically. Small differences (a few percent) are normal. Always verify against your project's engineered design before fabricating.

Where are my saved jobs?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. We cannot see or recover them.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the About tab → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID; there is no subscription.
