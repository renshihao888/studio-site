Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation runs on-device. The only network use is the App Store purchase flow for the optional Pro unlock.

What is the difference between candela and lumens mode?

Peak candela gives center-beam illuminance using the inverse-square law (fc = cd ÷ throw²). Lumens + beam angle gives average illuminance across the pool assuming a uniform beam; real fixtures peak brighter at center. Beam angle is the 50%-intensity edge, field angle the 10% edge.

What units do the results use?

Illuminance in foot-candles (fc) and lux, beam pool diameter in feet and meters. Lux is fc × 10.76.

Where do the fixture and gel numbers come from?

Fixture defaults follow the published ETC Desire D60 Studio datasheet; gel transmissions are Lee figures via the authorised retailer SetShop. Every formula is shown on screen — verify against your fixture cut sheet and gel swatchbook before you commit.

What does Pro unlock?

Gel transmission correction (8 Lee filters), scrim attenuation (½–2 stops), dimmer percentage prediction, identical-fixture count, and the ETC Desire D60 Studio fixture library. Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription.
