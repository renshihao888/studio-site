Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every calculation runs on-device. The only network use is the App Store purchase flow for the optional Pro unlock.

Where do the tap drill tables come from?

Pro tap drill sizes (UNC & UNF 1/4"–1", metric coarse M6–M24) are cross-verified against independent published charts (LMS, Lincoln, Starrett Bulletin 1214, CSG, rivcut). Thread geometry uses the exact 60° basic profile formulas.

Are speeds & feeds recommendations?

They are exact conversions (SFM→RPM, chip load→table feed) from the numbers you enter. Always follow your tooling manufacturer's ratings and your machine's limits — material, tooling and rigidity vary.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I restore?

Open the About tab → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID; there is no subscription.
